# 🛡️ Phishing Campaign Report

**📌 Project Title:**  
*Simulated Phishing Attacks to Test Employee Awareness and Improve Security Training Programs*

**📅 Date:**  
April 2025

---

## 🎯 Objective

To assess employee vulnerability to phishing attacks and enhance the organization's security posture through targeted awareness and training initiatives.

---

## 📋 Campaign Overview

**🔍 Scope:**  
A series of simulated phishing emails were sent to employees across various departments to mimic real-world phishing attempts. The goal was to identify how many employees clicked on malicious links or submitted credentials.

**🛠️ Tools Used:**  
- **Gophish** – Used to design and deploy the phishing campaign, and track user interactions.  
- **PyPhisher** – Used to craft realistic phishing templates and collect engagement data.

**🧠 Skills Applied:**  
- Social Engineering Techniques  
- Email Security Analysis  
- Security Awareness Training Design

---

## 🧪 Methodology

1. **Planning & Template Creation:**  
   - Created a sending profile in Gophish  
   - Designed email templates mimicking real threats

2. **Target Selection:**  
   - Selected employees from multiple departments (anonymized for privacy)  
   - Created a user group in Gophish

3. **Execution:**  
   - Deployed phishing emails via Gophish  
   - Generated phishing URLs using PyPhisher  
   - Launched a campaign in Gophish for tracking

4. **Monitoring:**  
   Collected data on:  
   - Email opens  
   - Link clicks  
   - Credential submissions

5. **Post-Campaign Survey:**  
   Conducted anonymous surveys to assess awareness and understanding of phishing tactics.  
   - Collected usernames and password submissions for analysis (anonymized)

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Mandatory Security Awareness Training:**  
   Conduct quarterly training sessions focusing on:  
   - Identifying phishing red flags  
   - Best practices for email security  
   - Safe reporting procedures

2. **Interactive Workshops & Quizzes:**  
   Reinforce learning with real-life scenarios and hands-on activities.

3. **Phishing Reporting Mechanism:**  
   Introduce a simple “Report Phishing” button in email clients.

4. **Follow-Up Simulations:**  
   Schedule follow-up phishing simulations every 6 months to measure progress.

5. **Gamification:**  
   Encourage vigilance through rewards and leaderboards for prompt phishing reports.
